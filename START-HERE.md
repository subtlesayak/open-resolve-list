## Narrow the choices

Use [access](views/access.md) to compare free, mixed, paid and public resources, [type](views/type.md) for related categories, or [updated](views/latest-updated.md) for dated activity. Unknown does not mean unsupported, and stars are not a quality rating. Official resources stay first in every view.

The [legacy view](views/legacy.md) identifies explicitly archived or deprecated projects. They remain in the complete inventory for historical workflows. An old push date alone does not make a project legacy.

## Ask an assistant

Share the [tool-finder guide](TOOL-FINDER.md) and a concrete request, for example:

> Find tools for animated subtitles in Resolve Free on Windows. I need local processing, no subscription and simple installation. Compare up to three options, link to the creators, and flag unverified requirements.

## Share first-hand experience

Use the [resource submission form](https://github.com/subtlesayak/subtle-resolve-list/issues/new?template=resource.yml) to explain what worked, the exact setup you used, limitations and any affiliation. Mark “Not tested” when appropriate. A tool becomes a recommended starting choice only after a maintainer reviews evidence of contributor experience; discovery alone does not qualify it.
