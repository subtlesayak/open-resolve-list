Explore a print-film look

All workflows

Expected result

An editable print-film treatment with a documented input and output pipeline.

Setup

A Resolve project and a PowerGrade suited to its color-management pipeline. The legacy Kodak 2383 download includes Resolve 14–16 versions; current-version compatibility is not established here.

Steps

  1. Choose a PowerGrade that matches your project’s color-management workflow; compare YRGB and color-managed alternatives.
  2. Download from the original creator and read the included instructions.
  3. For the legacy Kodak 2383 PowerGrade, use Gallery Import and choose the extracted DPX/DRX file as described by the creator.
  4. Apply on a duplicate grade, check exposure and skin tones, then compare bypassed and treated output. Keep input and output transforms explicit.

Tools and references

  • YRGB Print Film Emulation PowerGrades

Alternatives

  • YRGB Print Film Emulation PowerGrades

    Compare the separate DaVinci YRGB print-film collection. Match its input and color-management instructions to your project before choosing a PowerGrade.

Limitations

A film-inspired look is not a guarantee of physical film reproduction. Avoid a second display transform. No preview image is reproduced without permission; use the creator’s samples.
