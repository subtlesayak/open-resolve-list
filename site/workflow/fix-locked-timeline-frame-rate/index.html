Fix a locked timeline frame rate

All workflows

Expected result

Move an edit to a timeline set to the required frame rate and check what changed.

Setup

Confirm the exact delivery rate first. Resolve treats 23.976 and 24, and 29.97 and 30, as different settings. Keep the original timeline as your reference.

Steps

  1. Create an empty timeline in the same project. In the creation dialog, turn off Use Project Settings and set Timeline Frame Rate in the Format tab before adding clips.
  2. Open the original timeline and copy the intended video and audio tracks together. Paste the edit into the new timeline at the matching start point.
  3. Compare the two timelines at cuts and near the beginning and end. Check sync, transitions, speed changes, keyframes, captions, and any track-level audio routing or effects.
  4. Export a short test in the required format. Inspect the exported frame rate and check motion and audio before using the new timeline for delivery.

Alternatives

  • Set the rate on an empty timeline before editing

    If the timeline is already empty, adjust its Timeline Settings before inserting clips and skip the transfer.

    Original source

Limitations

A different frame grid can shift edit points and change how mixed-rate or retimed clips play. Timeline markers, track settings, and complex effects may need separate attention. Keep the original until the new render passes review.
