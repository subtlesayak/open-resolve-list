Grade action-camera log footage

All workflows

Expected result

A model-matched log-to-display starting point, followed by shot correction.

Setup

This example is limited to Osmo Action 5 Pro clips recorded in D-Log M and DJI’s corresponding Rec.709 vivid LUT. Other models and profiles need their own documentation.

Steps

  1. Confirm the camera model and recording profile from the shoot settings. Separate Normal, HLG and D-Log M clips.
  2. Find the Osmo Action 5 Pro D-Log M to Rec.709 vivid LUT on DJI’s official downloads page.
  3. Follow the Resolve color-training guide to install and apply the LUT, keeping its expected input and output explicit.
  4. Compare a duplicate grade, adjust each shot, and check scopes and the target display. Do not apply this example to an unverified camera/profile pair.

Tools and references

  • dji-clip-color

Alternatives

  • Leeming LUT Pro 4 for Osmo Action 5 Pro

    A paid, camera-specific alternative. Its Action 5 Pro guide specifies its own capture and white-balance workflow and Rec.709 gamma 2.4 target. Use the corresponding Leeming LUT and its instructions; the DJI LUT steps above do not establish that workflow. This catalogue has not compared the results.

    Original source

Limitations

D-Log M and D-Log are different profiles. DJI clip color organizes metadata; it does not transform pixels. This recipe is an editorial starting point, not a tested setup or an Action 6 claim.
