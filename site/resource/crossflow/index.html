Community discoveries · Workflow app

CrossFlow

By Nablast

Moves Premiere projects into editable Resolve projects and returns graded clips to Premiere. Preserves supported timeline structures and renders unsupported effects through Media Encoder. Requires Resolve Studio 20+; Premiere/Media Encoder 25+ are needed for automatic preparation and return. Windows 10+ and macOS 12+ on Intel or Apple Silicon. Paid one-time license; trial is limited to 10 clips per sequence.
