    NVIDIA RTX 30-series+; 4 GB VRAM for up to 1440p or 6 GB for 4K. Windows/Linux launchers documented; macOS source support not established.

  • requirements — Source documented
    Original source · checked 2026-11-21

    Official CorridorKey 2.0 Python model/runtime for local chroma keying and foreground/matte output, separate from the commercial OFX package. Requires NVIDIA RTX 30-series+, CUDA PyTorch and FFmpeg. Ordinary video is display-referred; unclipped HDR uses the documented linear-EXR path. Custom license permits output work but restricts redistribution/services and requires plugin seats for organizations above its revenue threshold.

  • license — Source documented
    Original source · checked 2026-11-21

    CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 with additional terms. Commercial output is permitted subject to the license; organizations above US$20 million annual revenue need per-user plugin seats. Paid redistribution/API services and commercial integration have separate restrictions.
