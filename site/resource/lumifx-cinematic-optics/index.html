LUMIFX Cinematic Optics

By LUMIFX

OpenFX toolkit with bloom, halation, highlight roll-off, grain, vignette, denoising and optical/transitional effects. Requires Resolve Studio 18+, Windows 10/11, NVIDIA CUDA, at least 4 GB VRAM and 16 GB RAM. macOS and V2 are advertised as upcoming, not available releases; current numeric version is unverified.
