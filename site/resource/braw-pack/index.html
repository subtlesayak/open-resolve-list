braw-pack

By laurentchicoine

Experimental local BRAW archive and recompression utility. Rebuilds H.265 archives into usable BRAW while retaining camera metadata. Lossy: retain original footage until rebuilt clips are checked. Supports NVIDIA acceleration on Windows/Linux and VideoToolbox on Apple Silicon. macOS builds are unnotarized beta.
