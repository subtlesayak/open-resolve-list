braw-pack

By laurentchicoine

Experimental local BRAW archive and recompression utility. Rebuilds H.265 archives into usable BRAW while retaining camera metadata, and can make smaller BRAW files. Lossy: retain original footage until rebuilt clips are checked. Windows and Linux builds plus unnotarized macOS beta builds; Linux GUI and interactive Mac workflows remain incompletely tested. NVIDIA accelerates supported Windows/Linux paths; CPU fallback is slower.
