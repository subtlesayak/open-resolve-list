Version history

  1. 21.0.4 → 21.1

    Adds Krokodove shapes/3D, OpenPBR, DJI D-Gamut2/D-Log2, GoPro Log, Leica L-Log and Vivo transforms.

    Recorded 2026-09-08 · upstream date 2026-09-08

    • Adds Krokodove shapes/3D, OpenPBR, DJI D-Gamut2/D-Log2, GoPro Log, Leica L-Log and Vivo transforms.
    • Improves MultiText alignment and font previews; adds viewer-line lens calibration, OGraf animation controls and multi-template DRFX macro editing.
    • Adds external camera-track imports, reference-frame tools, deep-image previews, hard-mix blending, HTJ2K EXR input/output and DNG correction bypass.
    • Supports OCIO 2.5 and ACES 2.0, richer variable syntax, log-conversion alpha handling, matte objects and motion blur in deep Renderer3D, faster deep rendering and monitoring.
    • Drops Python 2. Mac now requires Apple Silicon and macOS 15. Includes stability improvements; see full notes for exact hardware and driver requirements.
    Source changelog

    Recorded 2026-09-07 · upstream date 2026-08-05

    • Windows handling of exceptionally long paths is corrected.
    • The Paint inspector’s settings tab receives a fix.
    • Additional reliability and speed improvements.
