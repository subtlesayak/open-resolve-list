Color / Film · OFX plugin

MONONODES LOOK / LAB / PRINT OFX

By MONONODES

Film-inspired OpenFX grading suite for Resolve Studio 20+. Includes Lab and Print; the higher product tier adds Color Shift and HDR. Supports Windows NVIDIA RTX and Apple Silicon Macs. Paid licenses with a watermarked demo.
