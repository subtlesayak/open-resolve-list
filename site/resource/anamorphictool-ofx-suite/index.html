AnamorphicTool OFX Suite

By AnamorphicTool

AnamorphicFX and AnamorphicUtility OFX effects for Resolve 17+: lens geometry, astigmatism, chromatic aberration, vignette, horizontal sharpening and alignment. Both components are listed as v1.1. Windows/macOS distribution; Metal on Apple Silicon, NVIDIA CUDA and CPU fallback are documented. Exact Resolve edition and release date are not established.
