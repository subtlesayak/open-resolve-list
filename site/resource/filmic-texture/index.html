Color / Film · OFX plugin

Filmic Texture

By Filmic Texture

Resolve OFX plugin for gate-scaled film grain, halation, MTF and scan dust in ACEScct or DaVinci Wide Gamut / Intermediate, with HDR support. Watermarked 0.1 trial for Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 26+ with Metal 4. The provider lists a paid license; Windows builds are planned.
