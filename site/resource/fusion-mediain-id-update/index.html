    Lua tool for repairing Media Pool-backed Fusion MediaIn links after moving projects or timelines. Scans clip compositions and optionally all timelines; Fusion effects and adjustment clips require Resolve 21.1.2+. Uses path/unique-name matching, can import missing files, and offers dry-run, undo and verification. Ambiguous matches are left untouched; Fusion Clips inaccessible through the API and Loader nodes are outside its scope.
