VocalSlice

By VocalSlice

Local Whisper transcription and text-based audio selection, exporting named WAV clips for editing. Standalone audio preparation for Windows and macOS. Version 2.6.0 is free and open source under GPL-3.0; no trial or licence activation. Initial model download requires internet.
